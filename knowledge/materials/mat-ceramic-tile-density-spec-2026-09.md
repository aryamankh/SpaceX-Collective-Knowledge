# Ceramic tile density spec

## Context

A wet dress rehearsal on inconel-718, while the fastener lot was under watch by the console. The run was configured to the current published limits.

## What happened

Pressure on the fastener lot moved outside the expected band within 26 s. The run was cut short. No hardware was lost beyond the item already suspect.

## Root cause

The fastener lot had accumulated wear that the periodic inspection did not resolve at the resolution needed. Teardown found the condition; the the teardown report records it with photos.

## What we changed

The torque spec was revised and a torque re-verification was added to the task list before the next run. Owner: AlbericTrancart.

## What we would do differently

Stop on the first excursion of the fastener lot instead of waiting for a second confirmation. Two data points cost more than the abort.

The ncr, telemetry export and teardown photos filed against this note. Related notes are linked from the domain index.
