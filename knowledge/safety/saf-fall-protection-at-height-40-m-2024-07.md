# Fall protection at height 40 m

## Context

A night tanking on pad-operations, while the lockout tagout kit was under watch by the console. The run was configured to the current published limits.

## What happened

Temperature on the lockout tagout kit moved outside the expected band within 33 s. The run was aborted by the operator. No hardware was lost beyond the item already suspect.

## Root cause

The lockout tagout kit had accumulated wear that the periodic inspection did not resolve at the resolution needed. Teardown found the condition; the the run log records it with photos.

## What we changed

The acceptance band was revised and a pre-run visual was added to the task list before the next run. Owner: shahar603.

## What we would do differently

Stop on the first excursion of the lockout tagout kit instead of waiting for a second confirmation. Two data points cost more than the abort.

## Evidence

The run log, telemetry export and teardown photos filed against this note. Related notes are linked from the domain index.
