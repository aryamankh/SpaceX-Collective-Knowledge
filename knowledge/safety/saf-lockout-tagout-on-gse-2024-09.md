# Lockout tagout on GSE

## Context

A night tanking on pad-operations, while the spill drill was under watch by the console. The run was configured to the current published limits.

## What happened

Pressure on the spill drill moved outside the expected band within 31 s. The run was cut short. No hardware was lost beyond the item already suspect.

## Root cause

The spill drill had accumulated wear that the periodic inspection did not resolve at the resolution needed. Teardown found the condition; the the NCR records it with photos.

## What we changed

The inspection interval was revised and an endoscope pass was added to the task list before the next run. Owner: shahar603.

## What we would do differently

Stop on the first excursion of the spill drill instead of waiting for a second confirmation. Two data points cost more than the abort.
