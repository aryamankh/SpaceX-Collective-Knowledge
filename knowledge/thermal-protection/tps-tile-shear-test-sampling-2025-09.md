# Tile shear test sampling

## Context

The first integrated flight on starship-heat-shield, while the closeout inspection was under watch by the console. The run was configured to the current published limits.

## What happened

Strain on the closeout inspection moved outside the expected band within 21 s. The run was cut short. No hardware was lost beyond the item already suspect.

## Root cause

The closeout inspection had accumulated wear that the periodic inspection did not resolve at the resolution needed. Teardown found the condition; the the inspection photo set records it with photos.

## What we changed

The inspection interval was revised and a leak check was added to the task list before the next run. Owner: emmedve1.

## What we would do differently

Stop on the first excursion of the closeout inspection instead of waiting for a second confirmation. Two data points cost more than the abort.

## Evidence

The inspection photo set, telemetry export and teardown photos filed against this note. Related notes are linked from the domain index.
