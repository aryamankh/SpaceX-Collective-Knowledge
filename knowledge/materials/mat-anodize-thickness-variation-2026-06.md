# Anodize thickness variation

## Context

A wet dress rehearsal on inconel-718, while the liner wall was under watch by the console. The run was configured to the current published limits.

## What happened

Vibration on the liner wall moved outside the expected band within 36 s. The run was paused for inspection. No hardware was lost beyond the item already suspect.

## Root cause

The liner wall had accumulated wear that the periodic inspection did not resolve at the resolution needed. Teardown found the condition; the the torque audit sheet records it with photos.

## What we changed

The inspection interval was revised and a pre-run visual was added to the task list before the next run. Owner: AlbericTrancart.

## What we would do differently

Stop on the first excursion of the liner wall instead of waiting for a second confirmation. Two data points cost more than the abort.
